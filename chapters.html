EVERY TUESDAY

She is extremely cautious. But once she grows close to someone, she becomes completely defenseless. “...Looks like that kitten finally found a kind owner.” Yunokawa Riku, a second-year high school student, and Hayami Suu, a beautiful girl famous at another school. The two, who originally had no connection whatsoever, meet in a rainy park because of an abandoned kitten. Crouching beside the soaked, empty cardboard box, she looked just like another abandoned kitten herself... Unable to leave her alone, Riku lets Suu use his bathroom. Although she normally “acts like a cat,” could it be that she’s become especially attached to Riku? Before long, she starts making herself at home in his room and even leaves her belongings there. Riku fears getting too involved with her, while Suu fears relying on others. Together, the two begin searching for a unique kind of relationship that belongs only to them. This is a slice-of-life novel about a boy and a girl who are both the kind of people capable of handling life on their own without trouble.
