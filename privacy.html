2. Information We Collect

The following personal information is collected in connection with the Services:

Information you provide directly

When you use our Services, you may provide us with your personal information after being informed of the privacy information at the time of collection. For example:

  • Contact information: When a user is considered a child in accordance with applicable law in user’s jurisdiction, we may need the user’s name and legal guardian’s e-mail address to obtain legal guardian’s consent depending on your jurisdiction. Moreover, when you participate in our events or promotions, we may ask for your name, phone number, address, email address and any other information required to deliver prizes and to respond to inquiries.

    Additionally, when you subscribe to the Newsletter, such as TOOREAD ads Newsletter or Canvas Newsletter, we collect your email address to send you updates related to advertising placements, events, and other relevant information.

    Furthermore, you may choose to provide your name, company name, and job title to help us tailor relevant business communications and better understand our audience.
  • Account information: When you create your own account and login to your account, we may need your date of birth, nickname, email address and password. Additionally, when you login to a third-party account or to your profile with us, we may ask for information such as email address, nickname or profile picture.
  • Survey information: When you participate in our surveys or opinion polls, we may ask for your name, phone number, gender and any other information reasonably required to conduct surveys and provide rewards.
  • Image Information: When you choose to use toon filter, we may collect photos and gather information about the images, including identifying objects, scenery, facial and body features. This information is used solely to enable the toon filter effect and detect inappropriate images.
  • Customer Care information: To provide responses when you contact our Customer Care, we may collect any information you choose to provide, such as your requests or inquiries.
  • Creator Support Information: When you use a creator support feature (e.g., Super Like), you may enter your nickname, the amount of support, and a message. This information may also be publicly visible to other users.
  • Identifiers: Unique personal identifiers such as device identifiers, cookies, pixel tags, mobile ad identifiers, online identifiers, IP addresses and other similar identifiers.

[For Creators]

When you use CANVAS, AD REVENUE SHARING PROGRAM, and/or Super Like, we may need to collect additional information:

  • Creator Profile information: Profile picture, bio, social media links, website and other voluntarily shared information.
  • We may request that your PayPal ID or Patreon ID be linked to the Service.
  • We may request your uTip ID to provide rewards or bonuses.
  • Creator Payment Information: Legal name, business name, address, tax ID, email and related payment processing information.

Information collected through cookies and similar technologies

We and third parties may use cookies, web beacons, pixels, tags, and other tracking technologies to gather statistical information, improve your experience, save preferences, and provide offers. These cookies may track activity across devices and websites over time.
