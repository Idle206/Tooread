Everything we create is made for readers. From captivating light novels to a smooth and immersive reading experience, we strive to deliver unforgettable moments to everyone who loves great stories.

WE ARE TOOREAD

TooRead was created with the goal of bringing readers engaging stories and the most enjoyable and seamless reading experience possible.

Since its establishment, we have continuously developed our platform and expanded a diverse content library featuring many different genres, serving the global community of light novel and comic readers.

From lighthearted everyday stories to captivating fantasy worlds, TooRead always aims to build a high-quality reading space where readers can immerse themselves in every story and fully experience each moment of the narrative.
